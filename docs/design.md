# Interface decisions

Assumed user: a driver or dispatcher comparing a planned North American trip. The decision is which of three routes has less forecast exposure under the assessment policy, with time as the final tie-breaker.

The map occupies the desktop workspace except for its action strip and any deliberately opened checkpoint drawer or drive preview. The Spotter / Route planner header belongs entirely to the 340-pixel sidebar (320 pixels at intermediate widths). Trip inputs collapse to a trip summary after successful planning and reopen with Edit trip. Routes and Weather are separate sidebar views; the forecast slider and legend never overlay the desktop map. Their selected buttons and route cards use one primary-color border, without an inset underline or left stripe. Keyboard focus remains visible.

Phones keep the top of the map clear. A bottom dock contains Trip, Routes, Weather, and Checkpoints, with 48-pixel minimum targets and 12-pixel labels. The 64-pixel dock adds the device's bottom safe-area inset; `100dvh`, viewport-fit, and content-resizing keyboard metadata account for the visible browser viewport. Route/weather sheets open upward above the dock, capped at 36dvh, with matching space reserved so the road remains visible. Successful planning and route selection close the sheet and restore focus to the route control. The trip form opens in a bounded bottom sheet with a sticky close control and Compare action. Settings, GPS, Fit routes, playback, and zoom sit at the map's bottom edge, and the settings disclosure opens upward within the measured map height. Rules is accessible there. The desktop header remains inside its sidebar.

The checkpoint drawer starts closed, supports captured pointer dragging, and closes below its dismissal threshold. Pointer movement paints only the drawer height, at most once per animation frame; the map keeps its settled size during the gesture. Releasing commits the reserved space and triggers one map resize/refit. This avoids repainting the forecast field on every pointer movement. Its handle also supports keyboard resizing and closing. Open/close motion uses a 220 ms ease-out transition; dragging follows the pointer without a transition. Table rows become labelled compact blocks on phones. Fit routes restores the geographic extent without planning again.

Simulate drive opens compact playback controls and starts a road-following marker, forecast clock, and weather field together. Pause, seek, speed, reset, and close remain available. Opening checkpoints closes playback; starting playback closes checkpoints and the mobile controls. This prevents stacked panels from consuming the map. The preview explicitly distinguishes estimated conditions from live tracking and driving time from a duty-aware schedule. Marker transitions and drawer motion respect reduced-motion preferences.

Font: locally bundled Inter Variable, with sans-serif fallback. Roles: page title 20/650; section title 16/650; body/control 13–14/400–600; supporting data 12/400; compact legend 11/400. No additional display fonts. Inter's variable font uses the declared weights.

Palette: ink #1e3235, secondary text #56696b, surface #ffffff, background #f4f6f5, boundary #dce4e1, primary #17634e, primary hover #104b3b, selection #edf5f0, focus #225cc7. Risk colors: Low #207258, Moderate #a66a09, High #d25b23, Severe #b12f42, No Travel #622a70. Risk is always also expressed as text; ordinal risk is not a probability.

Optional night palette: ink #e5efea, secondary text #afc0b9, surface #172426, background #101b1d, boundary #3c5049, primary #83d3b1, primary hover #a7e4c7, selection #203d32, focus #9bbcff. Badge text/background pairs are Low #9fe0c1/#173e32, Moderate #f4d189/#493710, High #ffc6a5/#4b2a1c, Severe #ffbdc8/#4e2531, No Travel #e1b4f0/#3f2848. Road and weather-field risk colors retain the original palette. Only raster basemap tiles receive the night filter. Light is the default; manual Dark or optional local-time Auto is saved per browser origin.

Two compact map controls expose settings and opt-in device location. Device fixes use a blue #225cc7 marker and accuracy circle; stale/offline/error fixes use a muted marker and explicit status. They are visually distinct from the green simulated truck. Settings and status remain small disclosures rather than another full map panel. Loading uses a rotating icon, an indeterminate bar, elapsed seconds, and cancellation; reduced motion retains useful text without animation or fake progress percentages.

Spacing: 4/8/12/16/20/24/28/32 px. Controls: 42–44 px high. Panel radius: 8 px; control radius: 6 px. Subtle elevation applies only to map overlays, city results, and help disclosure. Icons: Lucide, with accessible names on icon-only actions.

States: initial, planning, cancelled, invalid, failed, results, changed inputs, and all routes blocked. Changed trip inputs leave the previous result visible with an explicit update message. No provider failure can silently replace weather with zeros or a demonstration fixture. Existing results remain visible if a refresh fails.

Map provider colors and labels belong to the basemap; the application overlays follow this palette. The weather canvas uses the declared risk colors with bounded opacity and interpolation of underlying wind/rain/snow samples. It is a forecast visualization, not a radar image. Road segments show arrival-based risk while the field shows the slider's absolute forecast time. These distinct meanings and interpolation limits are explained in the Weather view. Forecast retrieval time is distinguished from model issue time. No interface state claims continuous automatic updates.

Rules is a separate document page at `/rules`, reached from the sidebar. It replaces the planner's optional method, policy, and About disclosures. A persistent desktop contents column uses the same sidebar width and header; on mobile it becomes a collapsed, normal-flow disclosure. The reading column is bounded to 848 pixels including padding, with existing type roles, divided sections, a threshold table, and optional ELD/exception details. Internal links use browser history, set the page title and focus, and retain the trip's in-memory state. No navigation or explanatory panel extends across the map.

The planner keeps retrieval freshness, route-specific explanations, and essential limits visible. Driving estimates are labelled; checkpoints explicitly exclude breaks and rest. Routes over eight driving hours prompt checking break timing; routes over eleven prompt scheduling rest or a driver change under standard U.S. rules. No compliance badge, invented driver clock, or automatic exception appears. Rules carries the scope and official regulatory sources.
